Our Project Overview

Our project is designed to demonstrate our knowledge, creativity, and technical skills through a practical application. By combining our individual strengths, we aim to develop a useful, accessible, and easy-to-use solution.

FarmCast is a web-based agricultural weather monitoring platform designed to help farmers stay informed about local weather conditions and make better decisions to protect their crops.

✦

Our Goal

Our goal is to apply what we have learned, strengthen our teamwork and problem-solving skills, and build an application that meets the needs of its intended users.
